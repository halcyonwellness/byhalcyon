Privacy Policy

Application: Halcyon Sheets Pull · Halcyon Wellness, Inc. · Last updated: June 30, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes how Halcyon Sheets Pull ("the Application"), operated by Halcyon Wellness, Inc. ("the Company"), handles data. The Application is a private, internal-use integration built solely for the Company's own financial reporting. It is not a public product and has no external users.

2. Data We Access

The Application accesses the Company's own accounting data in QuickBooks Online via Intuit's OAuth 2.0 API using the "Accounting" scope. This includes financial report data such as Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow, vendor spend, and budget-versus-actual figures. The Application performs read-only access and does not write to, alter, or delete any QuickBooks Online data.

3. How Data Is Used

Retrieved data is used exclusively for the Company's internal financial analysis, budgeting, runway modeling, board reporting, and finance operations. Data is not sold, shared, rented, or disclosed to any third party.

4. Where Data Is Stored

Financial report data retrieved by the Application is written only to private Google Sheets within the Company's Google Workspace environment, accessible only to authorized Company personnel. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored within Google Apps Script property storage associated with the Company's Google Workspace account.

5. Data Sharing

The Application does not transmit Company data to any external party. Data flows only between Intuit QuickBooks Online (the source) and the Company's own Google Sheets (the destination), both under the Company's control.

6. Third-Party Services

The Application relies on two service providers, each governed by its own privacy terms: Intuit (QuickBooks Online) and Google (Google Workspace / Apps Script). No other third parties receive Company data.

7. Data Retention

Report data persists in the Company's Google Sheets until overwritten by a subsequent refresh or deleted by the Company. Access credentials remain in property storage until revoked by the Company or by disconnecting the Application in Intuit.

8. Revoking Access

The Company may revoke the Application's access at any time through the Intuit account connections settings, which immediately invalidates its credentials.

9. Contact

Questions regarding this policy may be directed to Halcyon Wellness, Inc. at ac@byhalcyon.com.
